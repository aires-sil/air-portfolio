Workstation Restoration: Dell M4800

I restored a Dell M4800 to peak stability, it involved deep cleaning the High-CFM fans to reduce noise and improve operating temperature. It features 32GB ddr3 RAM (4x8GB) and dual mSATA drives (475GB + 1TB) all reseated to ensure functionality. This has given reliable daily performance.

Items Used

  • Magnetic screwdriver bit set
  • Containers for screws
  • Vacuum

Physical Setup

Screenshots

Challenges

  • Bios navigation non-functioning: Restoration achieved through only software + hardware configuration
  • Windows 11 unsupported and Linux program incompatability - Reinstalled Windows 10
  • Encountered stuck screws. Required attention to ensure no stripping or damage to frame.
  • Identified damaged + incompatible chargers - Dig through boxes of cables to find functional charger

Learning

  • Safe teardown and Reassembley of enterprise-grade hardware
  • Preventative Mainteneance: Restored factory-spec airflow and acoustic performance - deep cleaning fans and vents
  • Technical decision making: Balanced trade offs between OS choices
